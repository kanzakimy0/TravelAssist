# Reviewer Guidance — TASK-039 v2

Thank you for independently reviewing these Japan POI comparisons. Your task is to decide which of two places better fits the traveler scenario shown on each row.

## Before starting

- Use only your assigned `reviewer-pack-r1.json` or `reviewer-pack-r2.json` and this guide.
- Do not inspect TASK-038 files, `internal-review-map.json`, source code, or another reviewer's answers.
- Work independently. There are no trick answers and some pairs may reasonably be ties.
- The supplied source links are neutral identity references. You may open them when the identity or broad type is unclear.

## Answer every row

Choose exactly one:

- `A`: POI A is a better fit.
- `B`: POI B is a better fit.
- `TIE`: both are similarly suitable for the stated scenario.
- `NEITHER_SUITABLE`: neither place is a reasonable fit for the stated traveler scenario.
- `INSUFFICIENT_INFO`: the neutral cards and links do not support a responsible judgment.

Then choose confidence:

- `high`: the preference is clear after checking the available information.
- `medium`: there is a reasonable preference, with some uncertainty.
- `low`: the choice is tentative.

An optional short note may explain ambiguity. Do not add your name or contact details.

## Important interpretation rules

- Judge fit for the stated traveler, not your personal favorite.
- Use `NEITHER_SUITABLE` when the available facts show that both options are poor fits. Use `INSUFFICIENT_INFO` only when the evidence is not enough to decide suitability.
- Treat low walking tolerance and low crowd tolerance as constraints, not as a desire for walking or crowds.
- Do not infer live opening hours, current queues, weather, route feasibility, booking availability, or price inventory.
- If those missing facts are essential to your judgment, use `INSUFFICIENT_INFO`.
- Similar-looking rows may occur. Judge every blind ID independently without trying to identify repeats.

## Submission

Copy all blind IDs from your assigned pack into a fresh copy of `reviewer-response-template.json`, fill every choice and confidence, set your reviewer code and submission time, and return the file unchanged after submission. The project maintainer will record its SHA-256 before analysis.
